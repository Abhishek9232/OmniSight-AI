# Problem Statement

Online examinations have become an important method of assessment in educational institutions. However, conducting examinations remotely creates challenges related to assessment integrity, student monitoring, and reliable evaluation. Existing online assessment approaches may provide question delivery and basic grading, but they may not sufficiently combine examination management, monitoring, behavioral evidence, and performance analysis in a unified system.

AI-based proctoring can help identify events such as the absence of a candidate or the presence of multiple faces during an examination. However, automated monitoring can also produce incorrect or misleading alerts. Therefore, simply declaring a student as cheating based on a single detected event may not provide sufficient evidence for a fair assessment.

The problem addressed by OmniSight-AI is to develop an academic prototype for an online examination environment that combines examination management, basic computer-vision-based monitoring, behavioral event logging, and data-driven integrity analysis. Instead of treating individual monitoring events as definitive proof of misconduct, the system will collect relevant evidence and generate an explainable integrity-risk assessment that can support further human review.

The proposed system will provide a unified environment for conducting online examinations, recording relevant examination events, analyzing behavioral features, and presenting the resulting evidence through a teacher-oriented dashboard. The objective is to improve the availability and interpretability of examination-integrity information while keeping human interpretation in the decision-making process.
